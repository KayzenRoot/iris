## Seven unadopted reuse concepts

### CONCEPT-01 EXACT_BYTES_REFERENCE

Bounded hypothesis: Exact old bytes/materialization are candidate evidence only; demand M02 reuse class and exact M06 immutable revision/integrity receipt.
Failure boundary: Same digest never proves semantic identity, owner rights, accepted production or storage permission.
Future owners: M02/M06/M53/M54/M55; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-02 SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENCE_REFERENCE

Bounded hypothesis: Compare owner-qualified M02 semantic-reuse class with M04 IR revision and M06 causality instead of hash equality.
Failure boundary: Stochastic generation or changed creative policy can defeat semantic reuse even with matching bytes.
Future owners: M01/M02/M04/M06/M14; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-03 FACET_DEPENDENCY_SLICE

Bounded hypothesis: Study M02 impact cones, M04 versioned facets and M06 complete dependency indices for a possible minimum sufficient rebuild.
Failure boundary: Unknown/hidden provider input, stale reverse index or moving alias forces an explicit unknown state.
Future owners: M02/M04/M06; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-04 TEMPORAL_SHOT_AUDIO_SLICE

Bounded hypothesis: Study bounded per-shot/per-frame/per-sample reuse only after future source-qualified composition and synchronization proof.
Failure boundary: Temporal seams, audio sample drift, color continuity, omitted mixed ancestry or altered codec can invalidate a segment.
Future owners: M01/M02/M04/M06/M37; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-05 IDENTITY_DNA_PROJECTION

Bounded hypothesis: Study reuse of old character, spokesperson, voice or brand projection as a reference to exact M05 protected anchors.
Failure boundary: Changing DNA revision, protected identity, subject consent or cross-modal voice/face alignment can invalidate an old projection.
Future owners: M04/M05/M06/M53; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-06 EPHEMERAL_TOKEN_PREFIX

Bounded hypothesis: Study provider-local KV/prefix cache as disposable inference optimization only, never production/materialization truth.
Failure boundary: Model, tokenizer, template, context, tenant or rights drift can invalidate ephemeral state even when a prefix hash matches.
Future owners: M13/M14/M16/M17/M53/M54; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.

### CONCEPT-07 PHYSICAL_CAS_POINTER

Bounded hypothesis: Study read-only observation of content-addressable bytes but reserve retention, tiering and deletion execution to M55.
Failure boundary: An existing object, digest collision or stale pointer grants neither accepted media nor tenant access or GC permission.
Future owners: M02/M06/M53/M54/M55; status **UNADOPTED_SOURCE_ONLY**.
