## 2. Eight proposed non-adopted model-card evidence facets

### FAC01: Composite model package identity

A weight-only digest cannot prove tokenizer, adapter or code closure. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC02: Exact task and evaluation fixtures

Different task populations or leaked data cannot be compared as equal. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC03: M07 original hardware and runtime

A copied device name is not exact source-qualified hardware, driver, topology or runtime evidence. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC04: M08 bounded protocol and admission

A designed protocol does not constitute authorized benchmark execution. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC05: Original samples with units and timing

Reported latency or VRAM without raw provenance cannot establish measurement. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC06: Contamination, abort and uncertainty

Unknown telemetry or interruption must not become valid confidence. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC07: Independent M01 quality evaluation

A faster result cannot bypass M01 fatal defects, required human review or the accepted M02 master. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.

### FAC08: Freshness, invalidation and tenant rights

An expired card or a license label cannot establish current tenant rights. NONBINDING_UNADOPTED.
