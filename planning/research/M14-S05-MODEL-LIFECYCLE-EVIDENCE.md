## 2. Eight nonadopted lifecycle research facets

All concepts require original qualified owner evidence and confer no runtime, licensing or model release grant.

### LF01_EXACT_COMPOSITE_DRIFT: Composite package/weights/tokenizer/adapters/config/custom-code drift

A changed or unverified component in the S01 exact composite package must invalidate inherited model identity or require a separately qualified new version; an alias or filename never suffices. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF02_TASK_IO_CONTRACT_DRIFT: S02 typed task, input-output and capability drift

Changed task or typed input-output semantics invalidate unverified capability mappings; an existing model badge cannot silently update S02 task scope or M01 fidelity acceptance. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF03_EMPIRICAL_POPULATION_DRIFT: S03 dataset, fixture, evaluator, population and metric drift

Future M08 measurement and S03 results require exact protocol, dataset population and evaluator provenance; unrepresentative or stale evidence cannot be promoted as measured capability. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF04_HOST_BACKEND_COMPATIBILITY_DRIFT: S04 host, driver, operator, compiler, precision and resource drift

M07 and M08 owners must independently qualify actual host/provider/runtime revisions; changed drivers or competing loads revoke inherited compatibility claims rather than implying continued fit. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF05_LICENSE_RIGHTS_RETRACTION: Versioned license, consent, trust, supply-chain and tenant rights

Changed or revoked usage rights, model supply trust, provider terms or foreign-tenant scope require independently qualified M18/M53/M54 owner evidence; no automatic commercial-use grant exists. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF06_CACHE_COMPILE_AND_WARM_INVALIDATION: Cached weights, warm instances, compilation graphs and artifact invalidation

An expired or changed composite model or runtime must not retain trusted hot caches, compiled kernels or resident copies; only future qualified owners may define safe invalidation and fencing. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF07_DEPRECATION_ROLLBACK_HISTORICAL_LINEAGE: Deprecation phases, rollback receipts and immutable M02 lineage

Historical accepted M02 masters and M01 quality decisions remain reproducible and audit-readable despite future version retirement; rollback is separately versioned, reviewed and owner-authorized. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.

### LF08_CROSS_OWNER_APPROVAL_LEDGER: Owner-signed lifecycle state, conflict, quarantine and nonauthorizing advice

Future M09/M10/M11/M12/M15/M16 inputs remain advisory until owner-issued and source-verified; unknown, conflicting or revoked lifecycle evidence must default to a nonadmitting quarantine path. SOURCE_TAXONOMY_NOT_ADOPTED.
