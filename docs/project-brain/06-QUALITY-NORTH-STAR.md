# IRIS 1.0 — Extreme Quality North Star

Status: `ACTIVE_DISCOVERY`

## Invariant

IRIS exists to make the visual/multimodal output of the Hive ecosystem production-grade. A technically successful generation is not an accepted asset.

## Quality hierarchy

1. Identity/anatomy/geometry correctness.
2. Temporal and motion consistency.
3. Composition, lighting, materials and artistic fidelity.
4. Technical integrity for target medium/engine.
5. Reproducibility, provenance and repairability.
6. Performance/cost efficiency after the Fidelity Contract is satisfied.

## Progressive fidelity

IRIS may create cheap drafts and previews to search the solution space. Drafts are disposable evidence. Final masters must pass the relevant Quality Court and cannot inherit a lower acceptance bar from the draft lane.

## 8 GB rule

A low-VRAM route is allowed to take longer, offload more data, tile/chunk the workload, use a smaller compatible model or escalate specific stages. It is not allowed to silently lower the final requested quality target. If the target is impossible on available local hardware, IRIS must report the gap and offer an alternate execution route rather than fabricate success.

## No single-model dependency

Model choice is empirical and replaceable. The system measures quality, identity preservation, hardware fit, latency, stability, licensing and cost per approved output.

## Human review

Human inspection remains a valid and sometimes mandatory judge for master-grade visual acceptance. Automated metrics assist; they do not invent aesthetic certainty.
