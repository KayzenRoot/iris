# IRIS — Persistent Virtual Spokesperson & Digital Ambassador Direction

Status: `CANONICAL_PRODUCT_DIRECTION`
Version: `1.0-planning`

## Purpose

IRIS must be able to create and operate a persistent synthetic on-screen representative that can become the recurring public face of a company, product, channel or fictional brand while preserving identity and rights across repeated productions.

This is broader than “generate an avatar”. The target is a governed, reusable cross-modal persona.

## Core identity package

A virtual spokesperson/digital ambassador profile may include:
- persona_id and semantic identity lineage;
- canonical face/head/body proportions;
- skin/hair/eye/groom anchors;
- approved wardrobe language and reusable outfit families;
- expression range and microexpression profile;
- gesture, posture and mannerism profile;
- Voice DNA / approved synthetic voice identity;
- speech rhythm, prosody, pacing and emotional range;
- linguistic/personality style;
- brand role and forbidden behaviors;
- approved reference images/3D/voice/video;
- rights, consent and provenance records;
- localization rules;
- continuity and drift thresholds.

## Production targets

The same approved persona should be reusable for:
- commercials and product advertisements;
- technology/news/explainer videos;
- company announcements;
- product demonstrations/tutorials;
- social short-form/long-form content;
- recurring series/episodes;
- cinematic scenes and trailers;
- website/Web3D interactive appearances;
- multilingual dubbed/localized content;
- live/near-real-time avatar/streamer modes when later runtime modules support them.

## Consistency contract

Continuity is cross-modal, not merely facial.

IRIS must validate:
- face identity;
- body/proportion identity;
- hair/groom continuity;
- skin/material response;
- wardrobe compatibility;
- gaze/expression behavior;
- gesture/mannerism identity;
- voice identity;
- speaking/acting style;
- brand/personality role;
- temporal consistency within a shot;
- continuity across shots/episodes/campaigns;
- continuity across image/3D/video/voice.

Allowed variation is explicit. Outfit, scene, language, lighting, hairstyle variant, emotional performance and camera can vary within a governed persona profile without becoming identity drift.

## Film / commercial / music production direction

IRIS must support complete audiovisual productions, not isolated clips:
- concept and script;
- scene/shot breakdown;
- characters/avatars;
- environments/props;
- camera/lighting;
- animation/acting;
- dialogue/voice;
- music/score/song;
- sound design;
- VFX;
- continuity;
- editing/compositing;
- color;
- mastering/encode;
- localization;
- final delivery variants.

Short commercials and long-form film/series content share the same Production Graph substrate while using different production profiles and quality gates.

## Quality rule

A persona is not considered consistent merely because a face-similarity model returns a high score. Identity approval uses the M01 Fidelity Contract with cross-modal evidence and domain validators.

A recurring company spokesperson intended for public MASTER output requires stricter human review and identity/rights evidence than a disposable draft character.

## M02 implication

The Production OS/Graph must model the persona as a stable semantic identity whose visual, voice, motion and episode/campaign materializations evolve as revisions/variants without collapsing the persona into filenames or one generated image.

M02 S03/S04 must therefore support:
- persona branches/approved variants;
- campaign/episode snapshots;
- reversible experimentation;
- selective rebuild when script/outfit/voice/language changes;
- continuity links across productions.
