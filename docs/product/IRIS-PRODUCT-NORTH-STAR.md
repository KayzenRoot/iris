# IRIS 1.0 — Product North Star

Status: `PROPOSED_M01_FREEZE`

## Final product vision

IRIS 1.0 is a local-first, hardware-adaptive, multimodal production system for creating production-grade visual and audio assets for games, websites, brands, campaigns and narrative media.

The final product is not a collection of model launchers. It is a governed production environment that turns a creative brief into validated masters through:

`BRIEF → INTENT/CONSTRAINTS → PRODUCTION GRAPH → GENERATE/BUILD → EVALUATE → REPAIR → VALIDATE → PACKAGE → DELIVER`

## What the finished IRIS should feel like

The operator works from one Control Center. IRIS discovers hardware, available DCCs/models/providers and project context; proposes or executes a production graph; schedules background workers; creates candidates; evaluates them; repairs only defective regions/stages; and exposes evidence before promoting a master.

Normal Blender and ComfyUI production should not require a forest of terminals or permanently visible applications.

## Quality promise

IRIS targets extreme final quality while using progressive fidelity for efficient iteration. Final masters are governed by a Fidelity Contract and Quality Court, not by successful process exit or a single quality score.

## Primary product domains

- 2D/2.5D/3D game assets;
- characters, creatures, environments, props, materials, rigs and animation;
- isometric/high-angle game production;
- image/photography/product/brand/logo work;
- Web 3D/WebGPU assets and animation;
- video/cinema/VFX;
- voice/music/audio;
- digital humans;
- marketing/content/advertising;
- localization and adaptive export.

## Product surfaces

1. **IRIS Control Center** — projects, production graphs, workers, quality, hardware and assets.
2. **Creative Studio** — brief/reference/intent creation and production initiation.
3. **Asset Lab** — inspect/compare/repair/version individual assets.
4. **Quality Court** — evidence, judges, defects, uncertainty and approvals.
5. **Hardware & Workers** — VRAM/RAM/CPU/process control and background execution.
6. **Model & Workflow Lab** — qualified models/workflows and champion/challenger evidence.
7. **Production Graph** — dependency graph, variants, rebuilds and lineage.
8. **Delivery Center** — game/web/social/media packaging and provenance.

## HIVE / CORE / IRIS relationship

- HIVE remembers and retrieves governed project/context knowledge.
- CORE reasons, plans and coordinates cognitive work.
- IRIS creates, evaluates, repairs and delivers multimodal production artifacts.

Integration is contract-based and versioned. No system silently owns another system's canonical state.
